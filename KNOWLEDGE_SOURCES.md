### **OmniBuild** — Building Codes & Standards

#### Current Scope
- Permits & utilities
- OSHA, ADA, EPA reference
- Project logging
- **Does NOT substitute for licensed professionals**

#### Knowledge Gaps & Expansions
1. **Code Coverage**
   - IBC (International Building Code)
   - NEC (National Electrical Code)
   - IRC (International Residential Code)
   - Plumbing, mechanical, energy codes

2. **Standards Libraries**
   - OSHA construction standards
   - ADA accessibility standards
   - EPA environmental regulations
   - ASTM standards (where openly available)

3. **Procedure Guides**
   - Permit application processes (by jurisdiction)
   - Inspection schedules
   - Trade contractor coordination

#### Open Sources
- **ICC Public Preview** (Limited free access)
  - https://codes.iccsafe.org/
  - Table of contents and some sections
  - Premium access needed for full codes (licensing required)

- **OSHA Standards** (Public Domain)
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs
  - Construction standards (1926)
  - General industry (1910)
  - Fact sheets and QuickCards

- **ADA Standards** (Public Domain)
  - https://www.ada.gov/
  - 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design
  - Technical requirements
  - Illustrated guides

- **EPA Regulations** (Public Domain)
  - https://www.epa.gov/
  - Environmental standards
  - Lead, asbestos, stormwater regulations
  - State implementation plans

- **NFPA Public Access** (Limited)
  - Some codes available for free viewing
  - NFPA 70 (NEC) - view only

- **HUD Building Codes Resources** (Public Domain)
  - Residential construction guides
  - Energy efficiency standards
  - Accessibility requirements

- **State Building Officials** (Public)
  - Many states publish adopted codes
  - Amendment documents
  - Permit guides

#### Licensing Challenge
⚠️ **ICC, NFPA, ASTM codes are copyrighted** and require licensing fees. Options:
1. **Reference-only approach**: Cite code sections, explain requirements, link to official sources
2. **Public summaries**: Use government summaries and interpretations (OSHA, HUD)
3. **Licensing**: Purchase organizational license for code text ($$$)
4. **Open alternatives**: Focus on public domain standards and government guides

#### Recommended Expansion
- **Permit navigator**: City/county permit requirements (crowdsourced + official links)
- **OSHA safety library**: Complete construction standards in plain language
- **ADA compliance guide**: Room-by-room accessibility requirements
- **Trade guides**: "When you need a licensed [electrician/plumber/engineer]"
- **Inspection checklists**: Pre-inspection preparation by trade
- **Material standards**: Common materials, specs, where to source

## Licensing Summary

### **Fully Open (Public Domain / CC BY)**
- Government publications (NIH, CDC, FDA, FAA, IRS, SEC, etc.)
- OpenStax textbooks
- Some MIT OCW (CC BY-NC-SA - non-commercial OK for apps)
- Wikipedia content (CC BY-SA)

### **Educational Use (Fair Use / Limited)**
- Academic lecture notes (with permission)
- Course materials (some CC BY-NC)

### **Requires Licensing ⚠️**
- ICC Building Codes (copyrighted)
- NFPA Codes (copyrighted)
- ASTM Standards (copyrighted)
- Some medical textbooks
- Proprietary financial data

### **Cannot Use**
- Copyrighted textbooks without permission
- Proprietary medical databases (UpToDate, etc.)
- Commercial legal research (Westlaw, LexisNexis)
- Paywalled journal articles (unless OA)
