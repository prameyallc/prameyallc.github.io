The portfolio

Eleven apps. One idea. OmniMath (OmniMathematics) has been submitted to App Review; the other apps are in development.

AppForThe line it does not cross
OmniSalubFor people managing a conditionOmniSalub does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician. It keeps your own record so you can walk into an appointment prepared.
OmniDentFor householdsOmniDent is not a substitute for a dentist, and it has no FDA authorization. Do not act on chat or photo-note text — ask a dentist.
OmniDermFor anyone watching a changeOmniDerm never tells you what a mark is. It records what you noticed — photographs and your own notes — so you can keep a private series over time.
OmniRxFor anyone on a prescriptionOmniRx is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, calculate doses, check interactions, or identify medicines from photographs.
OmniLexFor solo and small-firm attorneysOmniLex does not represent you, give legal advice, or file anything on your behalf. It puts public legal codes into plain language with every passage cited.
OmniBuildFor landowners and homeownersOmniBuild is not contracting, engineering, architectural, or code-compliance advice, and it is not a substitute for a licensed professional.
OmniWealthFor people learning how money worksOmniWealth never recommends a product or an allocation, and it never connects to a bank.
OmniOpsFor the solo operatorOmniOps does not certify, audit, or grade a practice, and it never totals avoided cost. It is a journal and a cadence, not a consultant.
OmniMathFor students and self-learnersOmniMathematics is not a course, a credential or a tutor who can certify you. Check anything you will be graded on against your course text.
OmniAviaFor student pilotsOmniAvia is ground school on this device. It is not flight instruction, not an endorsement, and not a substitute for a certified flight instructor or an examiner.
OmniPhysicsFor the curiousOmniPhysics is an educational sandbox. It does not replace a laboratory, a degree, or an instructor.
