Brand

The site and the apps should read as one line.

System fonts. Dark tokens. Each app's real OHBrand accent. No third-party type, no stock illustration, no fabricated screenshots.

App accents

These are the accents on the live landing cards, not the privacy-hub dots. Keep them in src/apps.json.

Icons

256 px PNGs, generated from each app's OHBrand accent. Do not replace them with a different mark.

Copy rules

  • No outcome claims.
  • No professional-role claims. OmniDent does not get a blanket “does not diagnose” line; its published policy withdrew that sentence.
  • No fabricated numbers.
  • Honest availability: “In development” until an app is submitted, “Submitted to App Review” once it has been submitted, and “On the App Store” only when store_url is a real App Store link.
  • No shipping download button. No launch waitlist. No mailing list.
