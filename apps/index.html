The portfolio

Eleven apps. One idea.

Each takes a field where expertise is expensive and asks the same question: what does the professional actually know — and how much of it can you simply be handed?

For solo and small-firm attorneys

The text of the law already belongs to everyone — it is simply unreadable. OmniLex puts public legal codes into plain language with every passage cited, so you arrive at a consultation already knowing the shape of your question.

  • Public codes, cited
  • Plain-language guides
  • Private document vault

OmniBuild
For landowners and homeowners

Building means permits, utility connections, inspections and trades — a maze that is mostly procedure, not expertise. OmniBuild maps what applies to your project, with the public standards cited, and is clear about which parts genuinely need a licensed professional.

  • Permits & utilities
  • OSHA · ADA · EPA reference
  • Project log
