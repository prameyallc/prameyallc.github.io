OmniRx

For anyone on a prescription

What a class of medicine is for, in plain language drawn from published labelling — plus a simple daily check and refill dates, so you reach the pharmacy counter knowing what to ask.

Write to us about OmniRx Privacy policy Consumer health data policy

OmniRx explains published labelling in ordinary language and lets you keep a simple daily check and refill dates. It does not take the place of a pharmacist or a prescriber.

  • Plain-language reference
  • Daily check
  • Refill dates
The line it does not cross

OmniRx is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, calculate doses, check interactions, or identify medicines from photographs.
