OmniDerm

For anyone watching a change

A private journal for your skin. Photograph the same spot across months and keep the series together, in your own words. The app records what you noticed — it never tells you what it is.

Write to us about OmniDerm Privacy policy Consumer health data policy

OmniDerm is a journal you keep: photographs of the same spot, notes in your own words, and a record you can take to an appointment. It does not name a mark and it does not tell you whether to see a clinician.

  • Photo timeline
  • Your own notes
  • Visit prep
The line it does not cross

OmniDerm never tells you what a mark is. It records what you noticed — photographs and your own notes — so you can keep a private series over time.
