OmniSalub

For people managing a condition

The record a clinic keeps about you — readings, history, what to raise next visit — kept on your phone instead of behind a portal login. Walk into the appointment prepared rather than trying to remember.

Write to us about OmniSalub Privacy policy Consumer health data policy

OmniSalub is a place to keep the readings you take, the history you already know, and the questions worth raising next time. It runs on your device. It does not connect you to a clinic and it does not speak for a clinician.

  • Readings from your own cuff
  • Visit pack
  • Imaging history
  • iPhone
  • Mac
  • Watch
The line it does not cross

OmniSalub does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician. It keeps your own record so you can walk into an appointment prepared.

This app can download AI model weights from Hugging Face when you tap to install them in Settings. That request is for a model file; it does not send your content anywhere. The privacy policy is the source for that sentence.

Availability is posted on this page when an App Store link exists. Writing to us does not add you to a mailing list — that inbox is read by a person.
