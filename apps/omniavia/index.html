OmniAvia

For student pilots

Ground school built around the FAA's own Airman Certification Standards — the twelve areas of operation, each broken into what you must know, what can go wrong, and what you must do. The regulation itself is always one tap away.

Write to us about OmniAvia Privacy policy

OmniAvia is ACS ground school on this device: the areas of operation, the knowledge, the risks, the procedures, and the text of 14 CFR in language you can actually read. It does not fly the aeroplane and it does not sign a logbook.

  • Every ACS area
  • 14 CFR in plain language
  • Go / no-go reasoning
The line it does not cross

OmniAvia is ground school on this device. It is not flight instruction, not an endorsement, and not a substitute for a certified flight instructor or an examiner.
