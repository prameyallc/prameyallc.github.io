OmniPhysics

For the curious

Physics you can experiment with rather than only read. Change a value in the sandbox and watch what it does — then follow the reasoning, step by step, from mechanics through to modern physics.

Write to us about OmniPhysics Privacy policy

OmniPhysics is a sandbox and a set of worked explanations. You change a value, you watch what happens, and you can follow the reasoning that sits under it.

  • Sandbox lab
  • Worked reasoning
  • Concept map
The line it does not cross

OmniPhysics is an educational sandbox. It does not replace a laboratory, a degree, or an instructor.
