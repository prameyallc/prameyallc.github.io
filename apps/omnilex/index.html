OmniLex

For solo and small-firm attorneys

The text of the law already belongs to everyone — it is simply unreadable. OmniLex puts public legal codes into plain language with every passage cited, so you arrive at a consultation already knowing the shape of your question.

Write to us about OmniLex Privacy policy

OmniLex is a way to read public legal text with the citation attached. It is preparation for a conversation with a lawyer, not a substitute for one.

  • Public codes, cited
  • Plain-language guides
  • Private document vault
The line it does not cross

OmniLex does not represent you, give legal advice, or file anything on your behalf. It puts public legal codes into plain language with every passage cited.

This app can download AI model weights from Hugging Face when you tap to install them in Settings. That request is for a model file; it does not send your content anywhere. The privacy policy is the source for that sentence.
