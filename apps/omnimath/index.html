OmniMath

For students and self-learners

Published as OmniMathematics.

Discrete math for computer science, and the math around it: topic packs with a cited source under each idea, a Codex of concepts, interactives, and a story tour of twenty chapters in six parts. No account, and no network needed to study.

Write to us about OmniMath Privacy policy

OmniMathematics is a learning app for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch app. Everything you study ships inside the app, and your marks stay on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud. Ask answers from the Codex and the topic packs; an optional on-device model, downloaded only if you choose, can rephrase an answer and labels it as generated.

  • 20 chapters in 6 parts
  • Sourced topic packs
  • Interactives
  • Study offline
  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Apple Watch
The line it does not cross

OmniMathematics is not a course, a credential or a tutor who can certify you. Check anything you will be graded on against your course text.

OmniMathematics can download an optional on-device Ask model (about 350 MB) from Hugging Face, only after you choose “Download (about 350 MB)” in Ask or turn on “On-device Ask model” in Settings. That request is for a model file; it does not send your questions or your marks anywhere. The privacy policy is the source for that sentence.
