OmniBuild

For landowners and homeowners

Building means permits, utility connections, inspections and trades — a maze that is mostly procedure, not expertise. OmniBuild maps what applies to your project, with the public standards cited, and is clear about which parts genuinely need a licensed professional.

OmniBuild is a map of procedure: permits, utilities, inspections, trades, and the public standards that sit behind them. Where a licence is required, it says so.
