How these apps are built

The paragraph below is quoted from the published privacy hub. It is the source of truth for architecture claims on this company site. We do not paraphrase it into a stronger or weaker statement.

Prameya apps run their analysis on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your content, and we do not keep user profiles. Some apps can download an optional AI model file from Hugging Face, and only after you choose to; each app's own policy says whether it does and when. That request is for a model file; it does not send your content anywhere.

Where an app touches health information, US state law requires a separate consumer health data policy. Those are linked above and from each app's policy.

Source: prameyallc.github.io/privacy. OmniOps is one of the eleven apps in this public portfolio and has a published policy on the privacy hub.

What this company site does not collect

This site is static files on GitHub Pages. It does not set a marketing cookie, does not load analytics, and does not load fonts or scripts from anyone but GitHub. If you write to {{email}}, we have your email address and whatever you put in the message. Support correspondence is not a mailing list.

Do not send photographs of your skin, medical records, or medication lists to that inbox. We do not need them to support the apps, and we have no special inbox for health files.

Four health apps

OmniSalub, OmniDent, OmniDerm and OmniRx each have a separate consumer health data policy, required by Washington's My Health My Data Act and linked distinctly — not as a section inside the main policy.

Claims this site will not make

OmniDent's published policy of 24 August 2026 withdrew a blanket “the app does not diagnose” sentence, because on-device chat is not filtered and can name a condition. This company site follows that policy on the OmniDent page. Do not copy a slogan over a policy.

OmniDerm's shipping build does not assess a photograph of your skin. The journal records what you noticed. That is why the OmniDerm page says the app never tells you what a mark is.

OmniRx's shipping build does not download a model, does not take photographs, and does not diagnose, calculate doses, check interactions, or identify medicines from photographs.
