Do you keep a mailing list?

No. The support page says so, and this site follows it. Write to {{email}} if you have a question. That inbox is read by a person.

Do the apps send my content to Prameya?

Prameya does not operate a server that receives your content, and does not keep user profiles. Some apps can download an optional AI model file from Hugging Face, and only after you choose to; each app's own policy says whether it does and when. That request is for a model file; it does not send your content anywhere. Read the privacy model and the app's own policy.

Is this medical, legal, financial or engineering advice?

No. The apps prepare, organise and explain. They are not a substitute for a licensed professional. Each app page states the line that app does not cross.

Why is the knowledge layer free?

“For everyone” would be hollow behind a paywall. Reference, education and safety content stay free. Paid tiers buy depth and convenience, never access to knowing.

Where are the privacy policies?

On a separate GitHub Pages project: prameyallc.github.io/privacy. This company site does not publish a /privacy/ directory, so those URLs stay stable.

What is OmniOps?

OmniOps is the operator journal: write the decision down, review it on a cadence, keep the habit. It does not certify, audit, or total a dollar you did not spend. OmniOps.

How do I ask for a deletion or a copy of data?

Most records live on your device and can be exported or deleted in the app. For a formal request, email {{email}}, name the app, and say which state or country you are in. We aim to answer within 45 days. Do not send photographs of your skin, medical records, or medication lists.
