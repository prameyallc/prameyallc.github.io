Company

Prameya LLC

A United States company that publishes on-device knowledge apps. We translate professional knowledge into language a non-specialist can use, with sources, and we stop where judgement belongs to a real professional.

Translation, not judgement

Most professional knowledge isn't secret. It is public, written down, and largely unreadable — scattered across codes and guidelines, in a register written for colleagues rather than for the people it describes.

What you actually pay a professional for is usually two things: the translation, and the judgement. The judgement is real and worth paying for. The translation shouldn't cost an hourly rate.

Each app takes one field and does the translation — carefully, with sources, and with a hard line at the point where genuine professional judgement begins. The result is meant to make you a better client, not a substitute for one.

Why on-device, and why sources

If the knowledge is for you, the record of you should not have to leave your device to be useful. Prameya does not run a user database. We do not keep profiles. Where an app connects to the network, the published privacy policy names the connection.

Where an app states something professional, it cites where that came from. Where a source is missing, it says so instead of filling the gap.

The knowledge layer is free

"For everyone" would be hollow behind a paywall. Reference, education and safety content stay free. Paid tiers, if they exist in a later build, buy depth and convenience — never access to knowing.
