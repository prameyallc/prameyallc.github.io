How it works

Three ways to access expert knowledge.

The reference library stays free. Personal features and unlimited usage are a small one-time purchase. Advanced features unlock with an optional subscription.

Knowledge stays free

Every app includes a free tier with full access to the reference library — the plain-language guides, cited sources, and educational content that make professional knowledge readable. No account required, no time limit.

That isn't marketing language. It is a constraint we build against. "For everyone" would be hollow behind a paywall. The knowledge layer — what the professional actually knows — stays free.

Per-app pricing

Each app is priced independently based on its complexity and the professional field it translates. Health tracking apps (OmniSalub, OmniDent, OmniDerm, OmniRx) start at $2.99–4.99. Professional knowledge apps (OmniLex, OmniBuild, OmniWealth) range from $7.99–14.99. Learning apps (OmniMath, OmniAero, OmniPhysics) fall in between at $6.99–19.99.

You only pay for the apps you need. No bundles, no subscriptions required — just buy the app and it's yours.

Why this model?

We chose this structure because it respects three commitments:

  1. The knowledge layer is free. If you just want to understand how legal codes work, or read plain-language medicine labeling, or learn physics — that costs nothing.
  2. Personal features are a one-time purchase. Your journal, your visit history, your tracking — that should be something you own, not rent monthly.
  3. Pro features are optional. Export, analytics, and convenience tools are valuable, but they aren't the core mission. They're available if you want them.
